Schedule Instructions

Enter shifts as 6-230 or 545-215. Paid hours appear below each shift and automatically deduct 30 minutes for every 5 hours of shift time. Changes are shared only after clicking Save Schedule. Weekly allowances default to the uploaded workbook's values for a new week.

On a new device, sign in with your email and password, then enter your existing PIN. Returning to a device with a valid authenticated session requires your PIN. The schedule locks after 10 minutes of inactivity. Signing out or an expired session requires email and password again. This PIN is a convenience screen lock, not a replacement for account authentication.
